01 — Overview

The short version

Your privacy is fundamental to how Clear Day is designed. This app was built on one principle: your data is yours, and yours alone.

Clear Day does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising networks, no third-party SDKs, and no servers of ours that your data ever touches.

No data is collected. No data is sold. This app is completely free. That's the whole policy.
02 — Data Storage

Where your data lives

All tasks you create are stored exclusively in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit private database.

  • Only you can access your data.
  • Your data is encrypted by Apple both in transit and at rest.
  • We have no access to your data whatsoever — not even in aggregate or anonymised form.
  • Deleting the app removes all local data from your device.
  • You can delete your iCloud data at any time via Settings on your device.
03 — Notifications

How reminders work

Clear Day may send you local push notifications to remind you of upcoming or overdue tasks. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device.

Notifications are never routed through any external server. No notification content ever leaves your device. You can disable notifications at any time in Settings, or within the app's Settings screen.

04 — What We Don't Collect

Absolutely nothing

To be explicit, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Usage statistics or analytics of any kind
  • Crash reports or diagnostics
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • IP addresses or network information
  • The content of your tasks or notes

This app contains no third-party trackers, advertising SDKs, analytics libraries, or any code that communicates with servers outside of Apple's own iCloud infrastructure.

05 — Children

Children's privacy

Clear Day does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, there is no special handling required.

06 — Donations

Buy Me a Coffee

The app contains an optional link to an external donation page at buymeacoffee.com. This is entirely voluntary and not required to use any feature of the app.

If you choose to donate, you will be directed to the Buy Me a Coffee website, which has its own privacy policy. We receive no personal information about donors beyond what Buy Me a Coffee may choose to share publicly on their platform.

07 — Changes

Policy updates

If this privacy policy ever changes in a meaningful way, you will be informed via the What's New screen when the updated version of the app is opened for the first time.

Any changes will be reflected with a new effective date on this page.
