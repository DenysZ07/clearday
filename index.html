What it does

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Smart Reminders
Get notified one hour before a task is due, and again the moment it goes overdue. Never miss a deadline.
iCloud Sync
Your tasks sync privately across every iPhone and iPad you own. No account needed beyond your Apple ID.
Priorities & Due Dates
Mark tasks as low, medium, or high priority. Set due dates and see what's overdue at a glance.
Instant Search
Find any task instantly. Search across titles and notes simultaneously as you type.
Filters & Sorting
Switch between All, Active, Done, and Today views. Sort by newest, due date, priority, or title.
Private by Design
No analytics. No trackers. No ads. Your data lives in your iCloud account and nowhere else.
Privacy

Your data belongs
to you. Fully.

Clear Day was built on a single principle: we should never see your data. Everything you create stays in your private iCloud account, encrypted by Apple, inaccessible to anyone but you. There are no servers of ours involved — ever.
